Why are my emails going to spam?

The rules Gmail, Yahoo and Outlook now enforce, the 8 most common reasons emails land in spam, and the fix for each.

QUICK ANSWEREmails usually go to spam because the sending domain is missing SPF, DKIM or DMARC, the list is old or wasn't opted in, volume jumped too fast, or people mark them as spam. Gmail, Yahoo and Outlook now require SPF, DKIM, DMARC and one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders, and expect spam complaints under 0.3%.

Check this first

Run your domain through the free email deliverability checker. It takes ten seconds and shows whether SPF, DKIM and DMARC are set up. In my experience, a missing record is the most common reason a small business's emails go to spam, and it's the easiest to fix.

The rules Gmail, Yahoo and Outlook now enforce

Google published these rules in 2024 and has tightened enforcement since. Microsoft started enforcing similar rules for Outlook.com and Hotmail addresses on 5 May 2025.

The 8 most common reasons, and the fix for each

  1. DKIM was never turned on. Very common with Google Workspace. Generate a key in the Admin console (Gmail → Authenticate email), add it to your DNS, and click Start authentication.
  2. Two SPF records. Adding a new tool often creates a second SPF line. Merge them into one.
  3. No DMARC. Add v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:you@yourdomain.com at _dmarc.yourdomain.com.
  4. Your sending tool isn't authenticated. Mailchimp, Brevo, Zoho Campaigns and others each need their own DKIM record for your domain.
  5. Old or bought lists. Old addresses bounce and some are spam traps. Clean the list and never buy one.
  6. Too many emails, too fast, from a new domain. Start with your most engaged contacts and grow volume over a few weeks.
  7. People mark you as spam. Usually because they don't remember signing up. Say who you are in the first line and make unsubscribing easy.
  8. The email looks like an ad. One huge image, many links, link shorteners and 'FREE!!!' subjects. Write like a person. Test subjects with the subject line tester.

FAQ

Why are my emails going to spam in Gmail?

Usually because your domain is missing SPF, DKIM or DMARC, you're sending from a new domain or IP too fast, people mark your emails as spam, or your email looks like spam (one big image, link shorteners, 'FREE!!!'). Check your domain with a deliverability checker first.

Why are my emails suddenly going to spam?

Something changed: a new sending tool without DKIM, a burst of emails to an old list, a spike in complaints, or a DNS change that broke SPF. Compare what changed in the last week and re-check your records.

Why do Google Workspace emails go to spam?

Most often DKIM was never turned on. Google Workspace doesn't sign emails with your own domain until you generate a DKIM key in the Admin console and add it to your DNS.

How do I stop my emails going to spam?

Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC; send only to people who asked to hear from you; add one-click unsubscribe; warm up new domains slowly; keep complaints under 0.1%; and write like a person, not an ad.

Is email marketing still worth it in India with WhatsApp?

Yes, for different jobs. WhatsApp is best for quick updates and conversations with people who already know you. Email is better for longer content, B2B buyers, international customers and building a list you own, and it costs far less per message.
