FAQ

Questions founders usually ask.

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)? +

AEO is the practice of making your brand the answer AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews give when people ask about your category. It combines entity building, schema markup, answer-first content and mentions on sources AI already trusts.

What's the difference between AEO and SEO? Do I need both? +

SEO gets you ranked in Google's list of links; AEO gets you named inside the AI's single answer. AI engines lean heavily on pages that already rank and are well-structured, so SEO is the foundation and AEO is the multiplier. Most brands need both.

Do you work with clients outside India? +

Yes. I work remotely with businesses in the UAE, UK, US, Singapore, Australia and beyond, with meeting slots that overlap your working hours. Invoicing can be arranged in your currency.

Can you build our website and the marketing around it? +

Yes, and that's the point of working with one person. Every website I build is fast, mobile-first and SEO & AEO-ready from day one, so you're not paying someone else to fix it later.

What does a custom ERP cost, and how long does it take? +

It depends on the modules you need. A focused MSME ERP (inventory, sales, invoicing, reports) typically goes live in 6-12 weeks. You get a clickable prototype and a fixed quote before any coding starts, so you know the cost upfront.

Do you work with small businesses and MSMEs? +

Absolutely. Many of my favourite clients are family-run businesses, manufacturers and local shops. Packages are sized for MSME budgets, and the free audit tells you where your money will work hardest.

How soon will I see results? +

Website, design and guerrilla work show impact within weeks. SEO and AEO usually show real movement in 3-6 months, and keep building after that.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend us? +

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Nobody controls AI answers. What I can do is fix the things AI tools look at, then show you a before-and-after test of the same buyer questions in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. More in AEO questions answered.

Do we have to stop using Tally if you build our ERP? +

No. Most MSMEs keep Tally for accounts and GST. The ERP handles orders, stock, production and dispatch, and sends the entries to Tally so your CA's work doesn't change. See Tally vs ERP for MSMEs.
