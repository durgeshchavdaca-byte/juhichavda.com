AI search29 Oct 2026

Reddit CEO says Google's AI Mode "does not drive traffic" as search visits fall

On Reddit's third-quarter call on 29 October 2026, CEO Steve Huffman said Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode are the main reason Reddit's search traffic is falling, because they push regular results further down the page. Reddit's shareholder letter says search referrals dropped by more than 1 million a week on average during October. Huffman said AI Mode does not send traffic to Reddit or, as far as he knows, to anyone else. Google has not responded to the claim, and Reddit will now test a logged-in-only version of its site in some markets outside the US.

What to do: Check the Search Generative AI report in Search Console to see how many of your impressions now come from AI Overviews and AI Mode, and compare clicks with last quarter. If clicks are slipping, put more effort into being the source AI answers name, starting with our AI visibility checker.

Sources: Search Engine Journal · Search Engine Roundtable recap

Google29 Oct 2026

Google Business Profile AI messaging reaches more businesses

Google's AI messaging for Business Profiles is showing up for more businesses, Search Engine Roundtable reported on 29 October 2026. Customers who find you on Search or Maps can text you from their phone's own messaging app, and a Google AI agent answers routine questions like today's specials or Saturday bookings, at no cost to the business. You can take over any chat, and questions the agent can't handle are passed to you. It only works for customers whose phone and network support RCS; Google has not published an official announcement or a list of countries.

What to do: The agent answers from what is on your profile, so update your hours, menu, prices and services in Google Business Profile this week. If messaging appears in your dashboard, test it from a friend's phone before you switch it on.

Sources: Search Engine Roundtable

India28 Oct 2026

Google brings ads in AI Mode to India and nine other markets

Google announced on 28 October 2026, alongside Alphabet's third-quarter results, that ads in AI Mode are expanding to 10 markets outside the US: India, Australia, Canada, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, the Philippines and Singapore. Text ads and Shopping ads can show inside AI Mode answers, marked "Sponsored", and they come from existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns. Google says Brazil, Mexico and the UK follow later in Q4. In the US it is also starting ads on follow-up questions within an AI Mode chat.

What to do: If you run Google Ads in India, check that your Shopping feed and Performance Max or Search campaigns are in good shape, because those are what AI Mode pulls from. For organic visibility in AI answers, run a quick check with the AI visibility checker.

Sources: TechCrunch · adgully · The Keyword (thekeyword.co)

Google25 Oct 2026

Google rankings swing again on 20-21 and 24 October

SEO trackers and the community reported strong ranking volatility on 20-21 October and again on 24 October 2026. Google has not confirmed an update for these dates.

What to do: Don't react to one bad day. Compare a full week in Search Console, and check whether drops are site-wide or limited to a few pages before changing anything.

Sources: Search Engine Roundtable

Google08 Oct 2026

Google's September 2026 spam update finished after almost 14 days

The update started on 24 September 2026 and Google's Search Status Dashboard listed it complete on 8 October 2026, about 13 days and 16 hours, its longest spam rollout so far and the fourth spam update of 2026. Google did not say which spam tactics it targeted.

What to do: If traffic fell between 24 Sep and 8 Oct, audit thin or mass-produced pages, expired-domain or third-party content on your site, and paid links. Improve or remove them rather than tweaking titles.

Sources: Search Engine Journal · PPC Land

ChatGPT04 Sep 2026

ChatGPT ads arrive in India: self-serve from ₹725 a day

Ads reportedly went live for Indian ChatGPT users on 27 August 2026, and the self-serve ChatGPT Ads Manager opened to Indian advertisers on 4 September 2026. Coverage reports a ₹725 minimum daily budget, no invite or insertion order, and two campaign types: Reach (CPM) and Clicks (CPC). Ads show to logged-in adults on the Free and Go plans only.

What to do: Test small (₹725-₹2,000/day for two weeks) with one clear offer, track leads not clicks, and keep investing in organic AI visibility. Ads appear in only a minority of answers. Plan your budget →

Sources: upGrowth · NewsBricks

Search Console03 Jun 2026

Search Console now shows your AI Overviews & AI Mode impressions

Google introduced Search Generative AI performance reports in Search Console on 3 June 2026, showing impressions in AI Overviews, AI Mode and Discover's generative features. It launched UK-first and later expanded; at launch it showed impressions but not clicks or queries.

What to do: Open your Performance report and add /ai to the end of the URL to see it. Track which pages earn AI impressions and give those pages clearer, answer-first intros.

Sources: Google Search Central blog · PPC Land

Google20 May 2026

Google: optimising for AI search is still SEO, and AI Mode passes 1 billion users

In May 2026 Google published guidance stating that appearing in AI Overviews and AI Mode relies on the same SEO best practices, with no special AI file or markup required. At I/O 2026 Google said AI Mode has over a billion monthly users and is expanding to nearly 200 countries. The May 2026 core update ran from 21 May to 2 June.

What to do: Don't chase AI 'hacks'. Strengthen the basics AI reuses: crawlable pages, clear answers, schema, fast mobile pages, and trusted mentions.

Sources: Let's Data Science · Search Engine Journal
