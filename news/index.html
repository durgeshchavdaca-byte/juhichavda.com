AEO & SEO news, minus the jargon

The search and AI changes that actually affect small businesses and startups, each with a 'what to do' you can act on this week.

Each story below has a plain-English 'what to do'.
Updated 6 Nov 2026 · by Juhi Chavda
Google05 Nov 2026

Google Ads adds California, South Korea and Vietnam to its AI ad label rules

Google Ads now asks advertisers who target California, South Korea and Vietnam to say when their ad assets were made or edited with AI. These places join the EU, India and New York, which were already on the list. In some regions, ads marked this way will also carry a visible AI label on the ad itself. Search Engine Roundtable spotted the change in Google's help pages on 5 November.

What to do: If you run Google Ads in India and use AI tools for ad images or copy, open the AI content label setting in your account and mark those assets. Google's help page below explains where the setting sits.

Sources: Search Engine Roundtable · Google Ads Help

Google04 Nov 2026

Google AI Mode now lets US customers book plumbers and other home services

On 4 November, Google's Search product VP Robby Stein said AI Mode in the US now shows booking and quote links for home services. The links run through partners: Angi, Thumbtack, Housecall Pro and Jobber, with ServiceTitan coming soon. Google has not said how it picks which businesses to show. Jobber says its users need a paid plan and a Google Business Profile linked at the same address.

What to do: This is US-only for now, but it shows where local search is heading: AI answers that end in a booking, not a visit to your site. Keep your Google Business Profile complete and add a booking or enquiry link so you are ready if it comes to India.

Sources: Search Engine Journal · Search Engine Roundtable

Google04 Nov 2026

Google AI Mode answers can now show up to four ads

A screenshot shared on 4 November shows four ads at the bottom of a single AI Mode answer. Until now one or two was normal, sometimes three. Google has not commented on the four-ad limit, but on its recent earnings call it said it plans to show more ads, more often, in AI Mode. Ads on follow-up questions are also coming in the US.

What to do: If you run Google Ads, watch your cost per lead closely this month as more ad slots open up in AI answers. Check that the extra spend is paying back with our ROAS calculator.

Sources: Search Engine Roundtable · Search Engine Watch

Search Console04 Nov 2026

Search Console now lets you filter by more than one device at once

The performance report in Search Console now lets you tick more than one device (desktop, mobile and tablet) in the same filter. Before, you could pick only one at a time. It was spotted on 4 November and Google has not announced it formally. A similar multi-select option for countries arrived about a month ago.

What to do: Compare mobile plus tablet against desktop for your top pages. If phone clicks lag behind, check page speed and how your title looks on a small screen with the SERP snippet checker.

Sources: Search Engine Roundtable

ChatGPT03 Nov 2026

OpenAI says ChatGPT now gets about one fifth as many searches as Google

In an interview published on 3 November, OpenAI's ChatGPT consumer product lead Adam Fry said ChatGPT's search-style queries are now roughly one fifth of Google's volume. He counts every question where ChatGPT goes to the web as a search. He also said weekly users grew from 1 billion to 1.2 billion in the last two months. This is OpenAI's own claim, not an outside measurement, and Google says its search volume is at an all-time high.

What to do: Ask ChatGPT the five questions your customers ask most and see whether your business comes up. Run a quick check with the AI visibility checker or follow our guide to getting on ChatGPT.

Sources: Big Technology · Search Engine Roundtable

Search Console03 Nov 2026

Search Console shows a sudden spike in clicks for some sites, likely a bug or bots

In the few days before 3 November, some sites saw a sharp jump in clicks in the Search Console performance report while impressions stayed flat. Most sites are not affected. Joy Hawkins said the click patterns on three sites looked like bot traffic, and others said the extra clicks did not show up as real visits. Search Engine Land and Search Engine Roundtable asked Google about it, but Google has not confirmed a bug yet.

What to do: If your clicks suddenly jumped, compare the same days in Google Analytics before you celebrate or change anything. Treat the spike as noise until Google explains it, and leave it out of any client or monthly report.

Sources: Search Engine Roundtable · Search Engine Land

Search Console03 Nov 2026

Google rewrites its help text for "Crawled, currently not indexed" pages

Google updated the page indexing help doc on 3 November. It now says Google does not index every page on the web, so "Crawled, currently not indexed" is a common message that does not always mean something needs fixing. It points you to the URL Inspection tool, and to Google's indexing issue form if you think the problem is on Google's side. The new wording does not mention page quality, even though Google has linked this status to quality before.

What to do: Do not panic over this status on thin pages like tags or old offers. For pages that matter, such as your main service and location pages, check them with URL Inspection and make sure each one gives a clear, useful answer that no other page on your site already gives.

Sources: Google Search Console Help · Search Engine Roundtable

Google02 Nov 2026

Google moves AI Overviews for brand searches into the right-hand panel

For many brand name searches on desktop, Google now shows the AI Overview in the right-hand column, the spot where the brand knowledge panel used to sit. SEOs first spotted it around 31 October and Search Engine Roundtable reported it on 2 November. In some results the AI Overview sits further down the page instead. Google has not announced the change.

What to do: Search your own business name on a desktop this week and read what the AI summary says about you. If it gets your services, location or hours wrong, fix those details on your website and Google Business Profile, since that is what the summary draws from. Our AI visibility checker can help you spot gaps.

Sources: Search Engine Roundtable

Search Console02 Nov 2026

Google won't promise click data in the Search Console AI report

The AI performance report in Search Console shows impressions from AI Overviews and AI Mode, but not clicks or queries. When a LinkedIn post suggested that data was coming, Google's Gary Illyes replied that the team wants to share more useful data but has nothing to announce yet. John Mueller later said that was an odd way to read the comment, so the door is not fully shut. For now there is no date.

What to do: Do not wait on Google for AI click numbers. Watch referral traffic in Google Analytics and ask new enquiries how they found you, so you have your own record of what AI answers send you.

Bing30 Oct 2026

Bing lets sites block AI training but still appear in Copilot answers

In a Bing blog post on 30 October 2026, Microsoft split its AI controls in two. AI training can be blocked in robots.txt or per page. AI grounding (using your page to build answers in Copilot, Bing AI Overviews and AI Mode) is set per page only, with a meta tag or header. A middle option, disallowextended, keeps your pages in Microsoft's own AI answers but keeps them out of the grounding tools Microsoft sells to other developers.

Both settings stay on Allow unless you change them. Microsoft starts reading them on 1 February 2027 and says normal Bing rankings are not affected, but warns that blocking grounding will cut your visibility in its AI answers.

What to do: Most small businesses want to be cited, so leave grounding on Allow and only block training if you have a reason to. If you use noarchive or nocache tags today, check Bing Webmaster Tools after 15 December, as those sites get a temporary grounding setting there.

Sources: Microsoft Bing Blogs · Search Engine Roundtable

ChatGPT ads get a wider card, multi-image ads open to all advertisers

Sources: Search Engine Roundtable · OpenAI

AI search30 Oct 2026

Gemini seems to have stopped tagging its links, so AI traffic may hide in Direct

Google started adding utm_source=gemini to links cited in Gemini in early September. SEO Natzir shared data showing the tags vanished around 28 September 2026, reported by Search Engine Roundtable on 30 October. Desktop visits still pass a gemini.google.com referrer, but his test of the iPhone app found clicks send no referrer, so they land in Direct in GA4. Google has not commented on the change.

What to do: Don't read a drop in Gemini-tagged visits as lost traffic. In GA4, add gemini.google.com as a referral source to watch, and keep an eye on Direct traffic to the pages AI tools cite most.

Sources: Search Engine Roundtable · Search Engine Journal

AI search30 Oct 2026

Google, Bing and OpenAI agree on one robots.txt line to opt out of AI training

On 30 October 2026 Google, Microsoft and OpenAI said they will honour a shared robots.txt token, __usage_ai-training, that tells AI companies not to train models on your pages. It comes from an IETF draft, and an Anthropic engineer told the IETF group the company has started adding support too. Rollout is slow: Microsoft says it starts on 1 February 2027, Google says early 2027, and OpenAI says "in the coming months". Google and Microsoft both say the opt-out does not affect normal Search or Bing rankings.

Two details matter. A User-agent: * rule does not count as this opt-out, so you have to name the token. And Google will treat it like blocking Google-Extended, which also stops your pages being used to ground answers in the Gemini app.

What to do: Most businesses that want AI tools to recommend them should not opt out. Publishers who want to protect their writing can add the token now and stay in search. Check or build your file with the free AI crawler robots.txt generator and checker.

Sources: PPC Land · Google Search Central · Search Engine Roundtable

SEO trackers and forums picked up two rounds of Google ranking volatility in the week of 27 October 2026, with the bigger spike around 27 October. Google has not announced an update, so treat it as normal testing until it says otherwise.

AI search29 Oct 2026

Reddit CEO says Google's AI Mode "does not drive traffic" as search visits fall

On Reddit's third-quarter call on 29 October 2026, CEO Steve Huffman said Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode are the main reason Reddit's search traffic is falling, because they push regular results further down the page. Reddit's shareholder letter says search referrals dropped by more than 1 million a week on average during October. Huffman said AI Mode does not send traffic to Reddit or, as far as he knows, to anyone else. Google has not responded to the claim, and Reddit will now test a logged-in-only version of its site in some markets outside the US.

What to do: Check the Search Generative AI report in Search Console to see how many of your impressions now come from AI Overviews and AI Mode, and compare clicks with last quarter. If clicks are slipping, put more effort into being the source AI answers name, starting with our AI visibility checker.

Sources: Search Engine Journal · Search Engine Roundtable recap

Google29 Oct 2026

Google Business Profile AI messaging reaches more businesses

Google's AI messaging for Business Profiles is showing up for more businesses, Search Engine Roundtable reported on 29 October 2026. Customers who find you on Search or Maps can text you from their phone's own messaging app, and a Google AI agent answers routine questions like today's specials or Saturday bookings, at no cost to the business. You can take over any chat, and questions the agent can't handle are passed to you. It only works for customers whose phone and network support RCS; Google has not published an official announcement or a list of countries.

What to do: The agent answers from what is on your profile, so update your hours, menu, prices and services in Google Business Profile this week. If messaging appears in your dashboard, test it from a friend's phone before you switch it on.

Sources: Search Engine Roundtable

Google28 Oct 2026

Google tells advertisers AI search queries are now 3x longer

On the Ads Decoded podcast published 28 October 2026, Google's Shivani Mohan said search queries have grown 3x longer and one in six searches is now multimodal (photo, voice or video plus text). Earlier Google figures tied these numbers to AI Mode in the US, and the episode gave no baseline. Ads Liaison Ginny Marvin added that ads in AI Mode and AI Overviews are matched to both the question and the AI answer, and mentioned six new optional product fields in Merchant Center.

What to do: Rewrite your top product and service pages to answer the full question a buyer would type, like "best GST billing software for a 5 person shop", not just the short keyword. Retailers should fill every optional Merchant Center field they can.

India28 Oct 2026

Google brings ads in AI Mode to India and nine other markets

Google announced on 28 October 2026, alongside Alphabet's third-quarter results, that ads in AI Mode are expanding to 10 markets outside the US: India, Australia, Canada, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, the Philippines and Singapore. Text ads and Shopping ads can show inside AI Mode answers, marked "Sponsored", and they come from existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns. Google says Brazil, Mexico and the UK follow later in Q4. In the US it is also starting ads on follow-up questions within an AI Mode chat.

What to do: If you run Google Ads in India, check that your Shopping feed and Performance Max or Search campaigns are in good shape, because those are what AI Mode pulls from. For organic visibility in AI answers, run a quick check with the AI visibility checker.

Google introduced Search Generative AI performance reports in Search Console on 3 June 2026, showing impressions in AI Overviews, AI Mode and Discover's generative features. It launched UK-first and later expanded; at launch it showed impressions but not clicks or queries.
