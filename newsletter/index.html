Hi, it's Juhi. Here's what changed in search and AI this week, and what it means for your business. Three minutes, no jargon.

Google · 5 Nov

Google may keep an important page in results for a while after it breaks, but strips its snippet

Google's Gary Illyes said on LinkedIn that when an important URL starts returning a 4xx error, Search can briefly protect it from dropping out of the index. The example was the VLC media player site, which by mistake served a 418 code. Google kept the link, but showed only the last good title with no snippet and no favicon, because it could no longer see the page. Once the site returned a normal 200 response, he said it should recover on its own.

What to do: Do not count on this grace period. Set up a free uptime check on your home page and key service pages, and look at the Pages report in Search Console after any site change or hosting move so a broken page gets fixed in hours, not weeks.

ChatGPT · 5 Nov

ChatGPT ads switch to Target CPA and Target CPC bidding from 9 November

OpenAI emailed ChatGPT advertisers to say new campaigns get Target CPA (for conversions) and Target CPC (for clicks) from Monday 9 November. The old Max Bid and Max CPC options will be retired on 1 January 2027. Existing campaigns keep running as they are until then, and the automatic option is being renamed "intelligent bidding". With Target CPC, single bids can go above your number, as long as the average stays close to it.

What to do: If you run ChatGPT ads, set your targets from your real cost per lead or click over the last few weeks, not a hopeful number. Check your pixel or Conversions API is sending the right event first, and use our ChatGPT ads cost calculator to sanity-check budgets.

Google · 5 Nov

Google Ads adds California, South Korea and Vietnam to its AI ad label rules

Google Ads now asks advertisers who target California, South Korea and Vietnam to say when their ad assets were made or edited with AI. These places join the EU, India and New York, which were already on the list. In some regions, ads marked this way will also carry a visible AI label on the ad itself. Search Engine Roundtable spotted the change in Google's help pages on 5 November.

What to do: If you run Google Ads in India and use AI tools for ad images or copy, open the AI content label setting in your account and mark those assets. Google's help page below explains where the setting sits.

This week's 10-minute fix

Check your own email setup. Run your domain through the free deliverability checker. If DKIM shows a warning, your emails are more likely to land in spam, and turning it on takes about ten minutes in Google Workspace or Microsoft 365.
