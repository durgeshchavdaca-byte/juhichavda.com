FAQ

What are AEO services?

AEO services make your business show up as an answer in AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. The work covers how your business is described across the web, pages that answer buyer questions directly, schema, crawler access and mentions on trusted sites.

How is AEO different from SEO?

SEO gets your pages ranked in search results. AEO gets your brand named inside AI answers. They overlap a lot: Google says the same basics apply to its AI features. The extra AEO work is quotable pages, off-site mentions and testing what AI tools actually say.

How do I rank in ChatGPT?

You can't submit a business to ChatGPT. It uses what it can find on the web and in search results. Keep your details consistent everywhere, publish pages that answer buyer questions in the first two sentences, let OAI-SearchBot crawl your site, and earn reviews and mentions on sites people trust.

How long does AEO take?

Fixes on your own site can show up a few weeks after they're re-crawled. Being recommended for competitive questions usually takes three to six months.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No. Nobody controls AI answers, and you should be careful with anyone who promises it. I show you a before-and-after test of the same questions so you can judge the results yourself.
