FAQ

What is a custom ERP?

A custom ERP is business software built for your own process: orders, stock, production, purchase, dispatch and billing in one system, instead of forcing your team to follow a generic product.

How much does custom ERP development cost?

It depends on the modules and the number of users and locations. You get a clickable prototype and a fixed quote before development starts, so you know the cost upfront.

How long does it take?

A focused system with a few modules usually goes live in 6 to 12 weeks. Larger systems are delivered in stages so you start using parts of it early.

Do we have to stop using Tally?

No. Most MSMEs keep Tally for accounts and GST. The ERP handles operations and sends entries to Tally.

Should I choose a custom ERP or Zoho, ERPNext or Odoo?

If your business runs a standard process, a ready-made product is usually cheaper and I'll tell you so. Custom makes sense when your process is your edge or your team won't use a generic tool. My Tally vs ERP guide explains how to decide.

Will it work on mobile?

Yes. It runs in the browser on phones and tablets, so the owner, sales team and store staff can use it anywhere.
