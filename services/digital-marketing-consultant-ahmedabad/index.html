FAQ

Should I hire a digital marketing agency or a consultant?

An agency suits businesses that need a large team for ads, social and design every day. A consultant suits owners who want one accountable person focused on the few things that bring enquiries. Many MSMEs get more from the second.

What does a digital marketing consultant in Ahmedabad do?

Plans your online marketing, does or manages the work (SEO, Google Maps, website, AI search, follow-ups), and shows you what's bringing enquiries.

Do you handle social media posting?

My focus is search, AI search, websites and lead follow-up, which is where most MSME enquiries come from. For daily social posting I can plan it and work with your team or a freelancer.

Can we meet in person?

Yes. I'm based in Ahmedabad and can meet clients in the city. Everyone else works with me on calls and WhatsApp.

Is there a long contract?

No. Monthly scope, no lock-in.
