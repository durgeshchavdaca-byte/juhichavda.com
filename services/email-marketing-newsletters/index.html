FAQ

How much does email marketing cost in India?

It depends on list size and how often you send. Tools like Brevo and Zoho Campaigns have free or low-cost plans for small lists. My work is a fixed monthly scope for writing, sending and reporting, or a one-time fee for set-up only. You'll know the cost before we start.

Is email marketing better than WhatsApp marketing?

They do different jobs. WhatsApp is best for quick updates and conversations. Email is better for longer content, B2B and export buyers, and a list you own. Most businesses do best with both, and I can set up both.

Why do my emails go to spam?

Most often your domain is missing SPF, DKIM or DMARC, or you're sending to an old list. Check your domain with my free deliverability checker, then fix what it shows.

Which email marketing tool should I use?

For most Indian SMEs: Brevo or Zoho Campaigns for value, Mailchimp if you want the widest set of integrations. I'll recommend based on your list size, budget and the tools you already use.

Can you write the newsletter for us?

Yes. I write in your voice from a short monthly call or voice notes, and you approve every issue before it goes out.

Do I need a big list to start?

No. A newsletter to 200 past customers who know you often brings more business than 10,000 strangers. We start with who you have and grow it with consent.
