FAQ

How much does an SEO consultant in Ahmedabad cost?

It depends on the size of your site and how competitive your market is. I start with a free audit, then give you a fixed monthly scope so you know the cost before you commit. There are no lock-in contracts.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Technical fixes and Google Business Profile changes can help within weeks. Ranking for competitive searches usually takes three to six months of steady work.

Do you do local SEO for Google Maps?

Yes. I set up and improve your Google Business Profile, fix your listings on other sites, plan reviews and build pages for the areas you serve. Nobody can guarantee a top-3 Maps position, but these are the things Google looks at.

Is SEO still worth it with AI Overviews?

Yes. Google's AI features use the same basics as normal search, and searches where people want to buy, book or call still send clicks. I build pages that work for both.

Do you only work with businesses in Ahmedabad?

No. I'm based in Ahmedabad and happy to meet clients in Gujarat, but I work with businesses across India and abroad.

Will you write the content too?

Yes, I can write the pages and FAQs, or work with your team. Either way I review everything for search and for clarity.
