FAQ

How can a doctor get more patients from Google?

Start with a complete Google Business Profile, a steady flow of genuine reviews, a clear page for each treatment, and the same name, address and phone everywhere. Most patients choose from the Maps results, so that's where to start.

Is SEO allowed for doctors in India?

Doctors can share factual information about their services, qualifications and clinic. I keep pages informative and avoid claims, guarantees and testimonials that medical guidelines don't allow.

How long does local SEO take for a clinic?

Profile and listing fixes can help within a few weeks. Ranking in the top Maps results for competitive treatments usually takes three to six months.

Do you do SEO for dental clinics?

Yes. Dental clinics benefit a lot from treatment pages (implants, aligners, root canal) and from reviews that mention the treatment.

Can you help clinics outside Ahmedabad?

Yes. Local SEO works the same in any city. I work with clinics across India remotely.
