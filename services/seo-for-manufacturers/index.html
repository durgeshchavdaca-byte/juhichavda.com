FAQ

Does SEO work for B2B manufacturers?

Yes. B2B buyers research online before they call, and they search for specific products and specs. Pages that answer those searches bring fewer but much better enquiries than general marketing.

Is SEO better than IndiaMART?

They do different jobs. IndiaMART brings volume and price-shoppers; your own site brings direct buyers who chose you, and it helps every IndiaMART lead check that you're real. Most manufacturers should have both.

How long until we get enquiries?

Product pages for specific, less competitive items can bring enquiries within two to three months. Broader terms take longer.

Can you help us get export enquiries?

Yes. I set up pages for your target countries and the questions importers ask. See the international SEO service for details.

Do you write the technical product content?

I write it with your team. You give me the specs and process details, and I turn them into pages buyers and search engines understand.
