FAQ

Does SEO work for real estate projects?

Yes, especially for area and project-name searches. Buyers who search a locality or your project name are close to deciding, and a good project page turns them into site visits.

Should we rely on property portals?

Portals bring volume but charge for it and show your competitors on the same page. Your own pages bring direct enquiries and support every other channel.

Do you include RERA details?

Yes. Each project page shows the RERA registration number and links to the official record, which buyers and search engines both trust.

How soon can a new launch get leads from Google?

Project-name searches can work within weeks of launch. Area searches take longer and benefit from starting before the launch.

Do you also run ads?

I focus on SEO, AEO and the pages themselves, and I can work alongside your ads agency so landing pages and tracking are set up properly.
