FAQ

How much does a website cost in Ahmedabad?

It depends on the number of pages, features like e-commerce or booking, and whether you need copywriting and branding. I give a fixed quote after a short discovery call, so the price doesn't change halfway.

How long does it take to build a website?

A company website usually takes three to six weeks. E-commerce and custom features take longer. You get a timeline with the quote.

WordPress, Shopify or custom code: which is better?

WordPress suits most content sites, Shopify suits online shops, and custom code suits sites with special features or very high speed needs. I recommend based on what you need, not what's easiest for me.

Will my website be SEO-friendly?

Yes. Every site comes with clean structure, fast loading, schema, a sitemap and correct robots.txt, so it's ready for Google and AI search from launch.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. I train you or your team, and I build the site so common changes don't need a developer.
