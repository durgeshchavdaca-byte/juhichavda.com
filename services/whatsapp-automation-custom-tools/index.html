FAQ

Can WhatsApp be automated for business in India?

Yes. The WhatsApp Business app has basic automation like greetings and quick replies. The WhatsApp Business API, used through an approved provider, allows automated follow-ups, notifications and CRM links, following WhatsApp's rules on templates and opt-in.

Is WhatsApp automation free?

The WhatsApp Business app is free. The API is charged by WhatsApp per conversation through a provider, and tools like Zapier or n8n may have their own plans. I'll show you the running costs before we start.

Will automation make my replies feel robotic?

Not if it's set up well. Automation handles greetings, reminders and updates, and hands real conversations to a person.

What kind of custom tools can you build?

Quote and price calculators, customer and dealer portals, stock or order lookups, internal dashboards and AI assistants that answer from your documents. If your team does it by hand every day, it can usually be a tool.

Can this connect to my ERP or Tally?

Usually, yes. I check what your current software allows and connect it where it's reliable.
