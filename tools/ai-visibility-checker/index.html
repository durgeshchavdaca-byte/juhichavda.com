    FAQ

    How do I check if ChatGPT knows my business?

    Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity the questions your customers would ask, like 'best [service] in [city]', and also ask directly about your brand name. Note whether you are named, described correctly and linked. Repeat monthly, because answers change.

    How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT?

    Make your business easy to identify and trust: keep your name, address and phone consistent, publish clear answer-first pages and FAQs with concrete facts, add schema markup, allow AI crawlers, and earn reviews and mentions on trusted sites.

    Is AI visibility different from SEO?

    It builds on SEO. AI answers lean heavily on pages search engines already index and trust, so technical SEO and quality content still matter. AEO adds answer-first writing, entity consistency and third-party mentions that AI tools use to pick one recommendation.
