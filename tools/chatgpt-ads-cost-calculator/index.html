ChatGPT ads in India: what we know (Oct 2026)

  • Live since: ads reportedly went live for Indian users on 27 August 2026; self-serve ChatGPT Ads Manager opened to Indian advertisers on 4 September 2026.
  • Who sees them: logged-in adults on the Free and Go tiers. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education users don't see ads.
  • Minimum budget: ₹725 per day reported, with no insertion order or invite.
  • Campaign types: Reach (bought on CPM) and Clicks (bought on CPC).
  • How often: OpenAI reportedly expects roughly one in five user queries in India to show an ad.

Sources: upGrowth · NewsBricks · AI Daily Post. Figures come from launch coverage, not OpenAI's own pages — check Ads Manager for current rules.

How to read the numbers

Impressions = spend ÷ CPM × 1,000. Clicks = impressions × CTR (or spend ÷ CPC). Leads = clicks × conversion rate. ROAS = (leads × value) ÷ spend. A ROAS above your break-even ROAS means the campaign pays for itself.

FAQ

Are ChatGPT ads available in India?

Yes. Reports say ChatGPT ads went live for Indian users on 27 August 2026 and self-serve buying through ChatGPT Ads Manager opened to Indian advertisers on 4 September 2026. Ads show to logged-in adults on the Free and Go plans, not to paid Plus, Pro or business plans.

What is the minimum budget for ChatGPT ads in India?

Coverage of the India launch reports a minimum daily budget of ₹725, with no insertion order or invite needed. Always confirm the current minimum inside ChatGPT Ads Manager before you plan.

How much do ChatGPT ads cost per click or per 1,000 impressions?

There is no reliable public benchmark yet. You can buy on CPM (Reach campaigns) or CPC (Clicks campaigns). Use this calculator with your own bids, run a small test for one to two weeks, then plug in your real CPM, CTR and conversion rate.

Do I still need AEO if I run ChatGPT ads?

Yes. Ads appear in a minority of answers and stop when you stop paying. Organic AI visibility — being the recommended answer — keeps working and makes your ads more credible when both appear.
