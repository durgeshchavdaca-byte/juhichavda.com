    How the score works

    The score rewards subjects that are short enough for phones, specific (numbers, names, a clear benefit) and honest, and it takes points off for spam-style words, ALL CAPS, piles of punctuation and too many emojis.     FAQ

    How long should an email subject line be?

    Aim for about 30 to 50 characters. Phones often show only the first 35 to 40 characters, so put the most important words first.

    Do spam words in subject lines still matter?

    Less than they used to. Inboxes mostly judge your sender reputation and authentication. Words like 'FREE!!!' and 'act now' still make emails look like spam to people, which lowers opens and raises complaints.

    What is preview text?

    The short line inboxes show after the subject. If you don't set it, the inbox shows the first words of your email, often 'View in browser'. Use it to finish the thought your subject starts.

    Should I use emojis in subject lines?

    One relevant emoji can help a subject stand out. More than one usually looks like promotion and can hurt trust, especially in B2B emails.
