FAQ

What is llms.txt?

llms.txt is a plain-text Markdown file placed at the root of a website (yoursite.com/llms.txt) that gives AI tools a short summary of the site and links to its most important pages. It was proposed in 2024 as a community standard.

Does Google use llms.txt?

No. Google has said you don't need special files or markup to appear in its AI features, and its John Mueller said no AI system currently uses llms.txt. In 2026 Google added an optional llms.txt check to Lighthouse, but that is an audit, not a ranking signal. It's cheap to add, so treat it as a nice extra, not a strategy.

Where do I upload llms.txt?

Upload it to the root folder of your website so it opens at https://yourdomain.com/llms.txt, the same place as robots.txt. On WordPress you can use a file manager or an SEO plugin that supports it.

What should llms.txt contain?

A # heading with your site name, a > one-line summary, optional notes, then ## sections listing your key pages as Markdown links with a short description each.
