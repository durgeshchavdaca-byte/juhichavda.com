FAQ

How long should a meta description be in 2026?

Aim for roughly 120-155 characters (about 920 pixels on desktop) so it isn't cut off. Google may still rewrite it, so lead with the most useful information.

How long should an SEO title be?

Keep titles under about 600 pixels (usually 50-60 characters) and put the main keyword and your brand early.

Does Google still use meta descriptions?

Often, but not always. Google may generate its own snippet from your page when it better matches a query. A clear, accurate description still improves click-through when it is shown.

Do titles and descriptions matter for AI answers?

They help AI and search tools understand what a page is about, but AI answers quote the page content itself. Pair a strong title with a direct answer in the first paragraph.
